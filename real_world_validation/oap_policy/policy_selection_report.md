# Real-World Policy Validation Report: Tamil Nadu Old Age Pension Scheme (OAP)

## 1. Executive Summary
This document presents the official policy specification, ground-truth benchmarks, implementation-aware simulation results, and accuracy evaluation for the **Tamil Nadu Old Age Pension Scheme (OAP / IGNOAPS)** backtested against the **SOP Project Synthetic Population Simulation Engine**.

The Old Age Pension Scheme is Tamil Nadu's primary social security pension programme, providing direct monthly financial assistance to destitute elderly citizens aged 60 years and above.

---

## 2. Official Policy Specifications
- **Scheme Name**: Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS) / Tamil Nadu State Old Age Pension Scheme (OAP)
- **Nodal Department**: Social Welfare and Women Empowerment Department, Govt of Tamil Nadu
- **Targeting Unit**: Individual elderly citizen (Age 60+)
- **Monthly Grant**: ₹1,000 / month (₹12,000 / year per beneficiary)
- **Reference Period**: FY 2023-24
- **Official Ground Truth**:
  - Total Approved Beneficiaries: **3,580,000 elderly citizens** (35.80 Lakhs)
  - Total Annual Fiscal Outlay: **₹42,960,000,000** (₹4,296 Crore)
  - Benefited Elderly Share: **32.45% of total elderly population** in Tamil Nadu.
