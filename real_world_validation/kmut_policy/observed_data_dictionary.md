# Observed Data Dictionary: KMUT Policy Ground Truth

## Metrics Definition

- `applicants`: Total number of formal applications submitted (1.63 Crore / 16,300,000).
- `approved_beneficiaries`: Total number of eligible households approved for KMUT grant (1.16 Crore / 11,600,000).
- `payment_recipients`: Active payment recipients receiving monthly transfer (11,500,000).
- `total_expenditure`: Total official fiscal expenditure allocated / disbursed for full fiscal year (INR 137,200,000,000).
- `benefit_amount`: Monthly cash grant per beneficiary household (INR 1,000 / month = INR 12,000 / year).

## District Coverage
- District-level beneficiary numbers represent official departmental allocations across Tamil Nadu districts.
